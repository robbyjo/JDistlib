Frozen evidence

Accuracy compared with R and independent references

30,710 scalar values

Thirty-six distribution families were checked for density, CDF, quantile, tails, log probabilities and boundaries. Disagreements were adjudicated with 80-digit arithmetic and independent inversion identities.

Scalar results →

10,905 joint-law values

Copula, multivariate, finance and compound-count references use R packages, base-R constructions and analytic checks. Unreliable R regions are identified rather than copied.

Joint and finance results →

2,076 utility references

Density estimation, bandwidths, moments, normality tests, multiple testing, splines and optimization are checked against R and independent mathematical identities.

Math results →

Inference and engines

Posterior diagnostics, PSIS, solvers, scaled decompositions, provider paths, WELL44497b and CMWC4096 have frozen fixtures and state-transition tests.

Use the evidence in context

What the audit does not claim

Higher-dimensional quadrature, fixed-grid Fourier finance paths, every specialized sampler, all accelerator operations, and every extreme parameter regime were not exhaustively certified. CUDA tests were skipped on the audit host. Read the detailed scope and reproduction commands before generalizing a result.
